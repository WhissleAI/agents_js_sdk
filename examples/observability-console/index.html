The agent

The reply at three granularities: one word at a time as the voice says it, the sentence-by-sentence partial, and the finished turn. Anything a tool renders — an image, a clip — lands here too.

Tool calls

Name, arguments, progress, result, citations, and the earcon that fired. A tool that failed looks failed; one that timed out without saying reads unknown, because that is what the wire said.

Predictions

Every bet the pipeline places mid-call — where the sentence ends, whether that noise was an interruption, which tool to pre-warm — and how it turned out. An unresolved bet stays open; no outcome is invented for it.

Signal stream

The pipeline's own subsystems narrating themselves: endpointing, barge-in, language, entities, flow. The roster above comes from the stream's opening frame — lanes it declares but never fills stay dim.
